Lab Guide & Teaching Notes

SPM Chemistry • Form 4 & Form 5

Prefer learning by doing?

Play our interactive tutorial level with a live dimmed spotlight guiding you through each tool!

📺 Smartboard & Projector Display

Click the purple Fullscreen Toggle at the bottom right before projecting. The 3-column lab automatically centers beakers, seesaws, and large formula tokens so every student in the classroom can clearly read atomic quantities.

📘 Curriculum Alignment

Structured around KSSM Kimia Tingkatan 4 Bab 3 (Chemical Formulae & Equations). Covers diatomic synthesis, acid-metal reactions, precipitation, and thermal decomposition.

⚖️ Dual Visual Models

Use the left sidebar tabs to switch between the 3D Seesaw (mass-balance intuition) and Bar Graphs (exact discrete stoichiometric counts).

💡 Classroom Tip: Starting with 1

Equations default to 1 of each molecule. Emphasize to students that coefficients of zero do not exist in real chemical reactions: we adjust counts from 1 until both sides balance.

🌿 The Golden Law of Conservation of Mass

Atoms can never disappear, and new atoms cannot appear from nowhere. Whatever atoms enter as reactants must leave as products. When both sides match, the seesaw balances!

1 Look at the element trays on the left. Imbalanced elements glow red with the missing atom count.
2 Tap + on molecules to drop them into the beakers. Never try to break atoms out of a molecule: you can only add whole molecules!
3 When every element tray turns green, the reaction is balanced! If you are stuck, ask Prof. Beaker for a clue.
