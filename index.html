📺 Smartboard & Projector Display

Project the live simulation directly in the classroom. The centered 3-column layout scales element tokens, seesaws, and discrete bar counts so every student in the lecture hall can easily follow along.

📘 Curriculum Alignment

Structured around KSSM Kimia Tingkatan 4 Bab 3 (Chemical Formulae & Equations). Covers diatomic synthesis, acid-metal reactions, precipitation, and thermal decomposition.

⚖️ Dual Visual Models

Use the left sidebar to toggle between the 3D Seesaw (mass-balance intuition) and Bar Graphs (discrete stoichiometric atomic counts).

💡 Classroom Pedagogical Tip: Starting with 1

Equations always default to 1 of each molecule. Emphasize to students that coefficients of zero do not exist in real chemical reactions: we adjust counts from 1 until both sides reach equilibrium.

🌿 The Golden Law of Conservation of Mass

Atoms can never disappear, and new atoms cannot appear from nowhere. Whatever atoms enter as reactants must leave as products. When both sides match, the seesaw balances!

🧭 The 3-Step MINOH Strategy

1 M & I: Balance Metals (Na, Mg, Fe) and Polyatomic Ions (NO₃, SO₄, CO₃ as intact groups) first.
2 N: Balance Non-metals (Cl, C, S) next.
3 O & H: Balance Oxygen and Hydrogen last—they usually fall into place naturally!
🎮 Tap + on molecule badges to drop compounds into beakers. You can only add complete molecules, never split atoms apart!
🏆 When every element indicator turns green and equilibrium is reached, the reaction is balanced!
