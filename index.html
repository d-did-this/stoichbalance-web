The Master Scale

Welcome to STOICHBALANCE! Your goal is to balance the chemical equation so that the left side (Reactants) and right side (Products) weigh exactly the same.

😢 Uh Oh! Unbalanced!
Keep adding blocks until the Master Scale is perfectly level!

Adding & Removing

👇 To Add: Drag the shiny buttons from the top trays into the large dashed drop zones. You can also just click the tray button!
❌ To Remove: Hover over any block you've placed in the drop zone. It will glow red. Click it to delete it!

Bots & Puzzles!

🤖 Helper Bot: Stuck? Open the right panel and click "Give me a Hint!". The bot will pulse the exact screens you need to look at!
📋 Puzzles: The dropdown at the top lets you pick different puzzles. Watch the counter to see how many you've solved!
🛠️ Custom Mode: At the bottom of the dropdown, click "Create Custom Puzzle" to type your own chemical formulas and solve them!
